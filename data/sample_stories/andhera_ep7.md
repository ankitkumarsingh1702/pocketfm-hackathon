# ANDHERA — Episode 7: "Chhati Manzil" (The Sixth Floor)

*Hindi-English horror-thriller serial. Runtime approx. 22 minutes.*
*Previously: Naina traced the whispering to flat 6B — the flat that, per the
society register, has been sealed since the fire of 1998. Tonight she has the
key.*

---

**[COLD OPEN — SFX: a lift groaning upward, cable ticking. Then it STOPS. Silence. A single bulb buzzes.]**

**NAINA** *(breath tight, whispering)*: Chhati manzil. Sixth floor. The building only has five, Rohan. I've counted them from the road a hundred times. Five.

**ROHAN** *(on phone, crackling):* Naina, get out of that lift. Right now.

**NAINA:** The button lit up on its own. I didn't— *(SFX: the lift doors slide open onto dead air — no corridor sound, no city, nothing.)* ...There's a hallway. It shouldn't be here.

**ROHAN:** Naina—

**[SFX: the phone dies. A child's laugh, far down the corridor, then swallowed.]**

**NAINA** *(to herself):* Okay. Okay. Flat 6B. Just like the register said. *(footsteps, slow, on cold tile.)* Someone's been writing on the wall. In chalk. The same word. "Andhera... andhera... andhera..." all the way down.

---

**[MIDDLE — SFX: interior of 6B. A ceiling fan turns with no power. Naina moves room to room.]**

**NAINA:** Living room. Sofa under a white sheet. Dusty. There's a calendar — 1998, June. Everything just... stopped.

*(She walks. A long beat. Footsteps. A door opens. Another door.)*

**NAINA:** Kitchen. Empty. The tap is dry. *(tap squeaks, nothing comes.)* Bedroom. A bed, made. A cupboard. *(cupboard creaks open.)* Saris, folded. Whoever lived here folded their saris very neatly. There's a photo frame face-down. *(she turns it — pause.)* A family. Mother, father, a little girl. I don't recognise them.

*(She sits. Sighs. A long, slow stretch of nothing.)*

**NAINA:** Let me think. The register said sealed since '98. The society secretary — Mr. Kulkarni — he told me the fire started in a sixth floor that doesn't exist. Ha. And Mrs. Dsouza on the third floor, she said the same thing last week, and the week before, when I asked her at the milk booth. Everyone says the same thing. Sealed. Fire. Don't go up. Don't ask. *(pause.)* I keep coming back to it and it keeps being the same. Sealed. Fire. Nobody remembers the family. Nobody remembers the girl. I've written it all in my diary — three pages now — and none of it adds up to anything new.

*(SFX: fan turning. A clock ticking that has no batteries. She wanders again — footsteps, footsteps, a drawer opening, closing.)*

**NAINA:** More chalk here. "Andhera." Again. There's chalk in every room. I could photograph all of it but my phone's dead. Maybe I should just... go through the cupboards one more time. Yes. One more time. *(drawers, again. A long, uneventful search.)* Nothing. Same saris. Same photo. Same dust.

*(A slow minute of small sounds — nothing advances.)*

**NAINA:** I don't know what I expected. It's just an empty flat that shouldn't be here. Maybe Rohan's right. Maybe I've been chasing a whisper that's only in my own head.

---

**[CLIMAX — SFX: the chalk word on the wall begins, faintly, to be ERASED. Scratching, from the other side of the plaster.]**

**NAINA** *(sharp):* The writing. It's disappearing. Something's rubbing it out — from *inside* the wall. *(scratching grows.)* The photo. The little girl in the photo. She's not face-front anymore. She's turned. She's looking at the cupboard.

*(SFX: the cupboard door, which she closed, clicks and swings wide. A breath — not hers — exhales from inside it.)*

**NAINA:** There's someone in the—

**[SFX: everything cuts to dead silence. Then a small girl's voice, right beside her ear.]**

**GIRL:** You found us. Nobody finds us.

---

**[WEAK ENDING — the paragraph to optimise:]**

**NAINA:** Oh! You scared me. Are you the girl from the photograph? You must be lost up here. It's very late, you know. Come, let's go downstairs together and find your parents, and I'll ask Mr. Kulkarni about all this in the morning. It's probably nothing. *(SFX: footsteps walking calmly toward the lift. Soft, gentle music.)* Don't worry, beta. Everything will be fine. We'll sort it out tomorrow.

**[END OF EPISODE 7.]**
