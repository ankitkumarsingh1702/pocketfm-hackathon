## How to read these numbers

Fidelity is 60% load-bearing beat recall, 20% all-beat recall, 20% causal edge recall, computed in plain Python from a majority vote of three independent alignment calls. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

Three things this benchmark does **not** establish:

1. **A trustworthy upper bound.** `calibrate.py` reports a 100% ceiling, but the two passes it compares came out byte-identical — at temperature 0 with the same prompt, that measures API determinism, not extractor robustness. The real ceiling needs a perturbed second pass and is still unknown.
2. **That genre A beats genre B.** Sample sizes are 2–3 and the judge has demonstrated instrument noise larger than the gaps between genres.
3. **That a dropped beat is real.** Some are alignment artifacts. Every loss listed above is worth reading by hand before believing it — in an earlier run, one of two reported drops turned out to be present in the prose and mis-scored.

What it does establish: the pipeline runs end to end across a varied corpus without failing, no genre collapses, and the load-bearing spine survives in the large majority of runs.
